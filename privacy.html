Privacy

Privacy notice

How we handle information you send through this website.

Information we collect

When you contact us, we may receive your name, company, company website, country, email address, phone number, enquiry route, timing, optional marketing preference and any information you include in your message.

How we use it

We use this information to respond to your enquiry, assess whether we can assist, route the enquiry appropriately and maintain relevant business correspondence. If you separately choose the optional marketing preference, we may also use your contact details for occasional relevant TSS updates until you ask us to stop. We do not ask you to submit identity documents, banking details or sensitive records through the website.

Website analytics and cookies

With your consent, we use Google Analytics to understand aggregated website use, such as pages viewed and traffic sources. We do not send enquiry text, names, email addresses, phone numbers, CRM notes or confidential opportunity data to Google Analytics. You can accept or reject analytics when you visit the website.

Service providers

The enquiry form is processed through our website enquiry system. WhatsApp and email enquiries are handled through their respective service providers. Their own privacy terms also apply when you use those services.

Sharing and retention

We do not sell your personal information. We may share relevant information with professional partners only when needed to progress your request and where appropriate. We retain correspondence only as long as reasonably needed for business, legal or record-keeping purposes.

Your choices

You can ask what personal information we hold about you, request correction or deletion where applicable, or ask us to stop using it for direct communication.

Contact

For privacy questions, email info@thesmartysolution.com.
