Evaluating an opportunity

Questions investors should answer before engaging.

A credible evaluation goes beyond location or headline potential. Investors should establish the commercial objective, ownership position, planning or regulatory status, capital requirements, expected timeframe and who is responsible for execution.

  • Confirm ownership and authority to present the opportunity
  • Verify legal, planning, financial and technical information
  • Test costs, demand, timing and exit assumptions
  • Use qualified independent advisers before committing
