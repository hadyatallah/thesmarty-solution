What we do

From market ambition to the right commercial relationship.

We support growth by combining business development, strategic introductions and disciplined opportunity coordination.

01

Business development

Commercial planning, partner identification, market-entry support and relationship development for companies expanding across EMEA.

02

Commercial representation

Structured representation for businesses that need credible local access, consistent follow-up and an experienced point of contact.

03

Investor introductions

Carefully considered introductions between business owners and potential investors, based on strategic fit and stated interests.

Business owners

Prepare the opportunity before presenting it.

We help clarify the proposition, investor profile and purpose of the introduction. Where there is a credible match, we coordinate the initial engagement and follow-up.

  • Define the commercial opportunity
  • Clarify capital or partnership objectives
  • Identify relevant counterparties
  • Coordinate introductions and next actions

Investors

See opportunities with the right context.

We present selected opportunities from within our network and help establish direct communication with the business owner or project representative.

  • Relevant rather than mass-market circulation
  • Clear source and point of contact
  • Access to business owners and project stakeholders
  • Practical coordination during early discussions

Practical execution

We can build the structure around the opportunity too.

Not every problem is solved by another meeting. Where useful, we also organise the information and workflow needed to keep commercial activity moving.

Opportunity packaging

Turn available commercial, property or project information into a clear proposition with defined audience, qualification criteria and next steps.

CRM and follow-up structure

Design practical lead, customer, follow-up and activity structures so ownership and next actions are visible instead of sitting across separate inboxes and notes.

Operational workflows

Structure request intake, status tracking, ownership and escalation paths where a business process needs more control and consistency.

Our process

Four steps before a useful connection.

We don’t guarantee funding, transactions or commercial outcomes. We improve the quality of the opportunity and the conversations around it.

Understand

Clarify objectives, timing and the real commercial need.

Assess

Review whether the opportunity is ready and relevant.

Connect

Approach appropriate parties with clear context.

Coordinate

Maintain momentum, ownership and next steps.

Supporting services

Practical coordination around selected opportunities.

Where relevant, we can also coordinate property sourcing, residency-related support and bank account application preparation through suitable independent professionals and providers.

Clear professional boundaries

The Smarty Solution provides business consultancy, introductions and coordination. We do not provide regulated investment, legal, tax, immigration, banking or financial advice.
