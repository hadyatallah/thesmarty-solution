Privacy

Privacy notice

How we handle information shared through our website and business communication channels.

Who is responsible for your information

The Smarty Solution Ltd, Cyprus, is responsible for personal information submitted to our website or shared in our business correspondence. For privacy enquiries, contact info@thesmartysolution.com.

Information we collect

When you contact us, we may receive your name, company, company website, country, email address, phone number, enquiry route, timing, optional marketing preference and any information you include in your message.

How we use it

We use this information to respond to your enquiry, assess whether we can assist, route the enquiry appropriately and maintain relevant business correspondence. If you separately choose the optional marketing preference, we may also use your contact details for occasional relevant TSS updates until you ask us to stop. We do not ask you to submit identity documents, banking details or sensitive records through the website.

WhatsApp and other business communications

When you contact us by WhatsApp, email or another business channel, we may process your name or business name, contact details, the content of your enquiry and replies, message attachments you choose to send, and related delivery information such as message identifiers, timestamps and status updates. We use this information to understand and respond to your request, maintain relevant business correspondence, manage legitimate enquiries and protect the security of our communications. An enquiry does not, by itself, mean you have consented to unrelated marketing.

When we use the WhatsApp Business Platform, Meta provides the messaging infrastructure and our authorized service infrastructure may receive webhook events. Our current technical design uses Google Cloud services, including Firestore configured in the EU, for secure reception and review of relevant message events. Use of an EU database location does not necessarily mean that every service provider operation or transfer occurs solely within the EU. We do not ask you to send passwords, payment-card details, identity documents or similarly sensitive records in a WhatsApp message.

Website analytics and cookies

With your consent, we use Google Analytics to understand aggregated website use, such as pages viewed and traffic sources. We do not send enquiry text, names, email addresses, phone numbers, CRM notes or confidential opportunity data to Google Analytics. You can accept or reject analytics when you visit the website.

Service providers

The website contact form is provided by HubSpot, which receives and processes submitted enquiries for our commercial CRM. Email and WhatsApp messages are handled through their respective providers, including Meta for WhatsApp and Google Cloud for authorized webhook storage when the integration is in use. Their privacy terms also apply when you use those services.

Sharing and retention

We do not sell your personal information. We may share relevant information with professional partners only when needed to progress your request and where appropriate. We retain correspondence only as long as reasonably needed for business, legal or record-keeping purposes.

Your choices

You can ask what personal information we hold about you, request correction or deletion where applicable, or ask us to stop using it for direct communication. To request removal of data we control, see our data-deletion instructions. Some information may need to be retained where applicable law or a legitimate record-keeping obligation requires it.

Contact

For privacy questions, email info@thesmartysolution.com.
