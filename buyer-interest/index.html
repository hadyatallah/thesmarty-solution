Our role

Independent business development and introductions.

The Smarty Solution LTD is a Cyprus-based business-development and research company. We are not offering a public property listing, financial product or guaranteed investment.

We do not charge prospective buyers for an initial introduction. Our services are funded by our business clients, and TSS may receive compensation from them. We will not hide this relationship or pass your information to a specific property professional without the necessary transparency and permission.

The Smarty Solution does not provide regulated property, investment, financial, tax, legal or immigration advice. A suitably qualified professional should assess any particular transaction, title, financing or residency question.
