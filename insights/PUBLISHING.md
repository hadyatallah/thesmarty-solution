# TSS Insights publishing guide\n\nPublic files: `insights/index.html` and `insights/insights.css`. Future approved articles go in `insights/<stable-slug>/index.html`.\n\nRequired: title, slug, summary, category, author, dates, body, sources, image rights/alt text, disclaimer where needed, and `/contact.html` next step.\n\nReview facts and sources, confirm image permission, obtain final owner approval, then add the page and sitemap entry. Preview desktop/mobile and verify metadata, links and draft exclusion before deployment. Unpublish by removing the article route and sitemap entry. An approved article from another conversation must be handed over by pasting the final approved content and approval status into the active implementation conversation.\n
