How We Work

A clear route from business objective to action.

We first understand what the business is trying to achieve. Then we agree the right work, scope and next step before execution begins.

Our process

A clear route from objective to execution.

The engagement depends on what the business actually needs. We agree the next step before substantive work begins.

1. Understand the objective

Clarify what the business wants to achieve and why it matters now.

2. Establish what is known

Review the market, customers, proposition, timing, decision authority and current situation.

3. Identify gaps

Separate confirmed facts from assumptions and identify what is missing.

4. Define the engagement

Agree scope, responsibilities, boundaries, communication and any specialist support.

5. Execute controlled work

Carry out the approved commercial or systems work within the agreed authority.

6. Review and progress

Assess what changed, what remains open and the appropriate next commercial step.

Before execution

Make sure the situation is ready for the work.

Sometimes the objective is clear but the business, proposition or opportunity needs preparation first. TSS may recommend Commercial Readiness work before outreach or implementation. This is an internal conclusion based on the situation, not a visitor-selected service.

  • Confirm decision authority and ownership
  • Clarify the proposition, evidence and target market
  • Identify missing information or dependencies
  • Set a realistic scope, timing and next step

Start with the objective

We determine whether a process or system change is justified. Where an existing CRM or tool works, we look first at improvement, integration, reporting, automation or adoption.

Specialist support

Bring in qualified expertise where needed.

Legal, tax, immigration, accounting, property and other specialist or regulated matters may require qualified external professionals. TSS can coordinate around the commercial objective while each professional remains responsible for their specialist advice or regulated service.

Clear boundaries

Authority, responsibilities and disclosure are agreed before work starts. Representation does not by itself give TSS authority to bind a client, sign contracts, set prices or provide regulated advice.

Next step

Tell us what you need to achieve.

We will review the context and identify the most relevant next step.
