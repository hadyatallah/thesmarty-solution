Development Partnerships

The Smarty Solution explores opportunities to participate in development partnerships in Cyprus. If you are a landowner or developer, we welcome a conversation about whether there may be a fit.

Discuss a Development Partnership

What is antiparochi (αντιπαροχή)?

Instead of selling the land outright, a landowner can contribute a site and work with a developer who takes on the development. The landowner typically receives part of what is built. In Cyprus, this arrangement is often called antiparochi.

It starts with a conversation about the site and what each side wants to achieve. Not every site or prospective partnership is a fit.
