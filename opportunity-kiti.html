Residential development opportunity · Kiti, Larnaca

859 m² H2 development site with preliminary potential for 10 to 12 apartments.

A privately owned residential development opportunity in Kiti, available for developers and investors to assess through an outright sale, consideration-in-kind structure or another suitable development arrangement.

Request developer informationEnquire on WhatsApp

Opportunity overview

A development proposition, not only a land listing.

The site comprises 859 m² within the H2 residential planning zone. A preliminary feasibility assessment has already tested two apartment-development configurations, giving prospective developers a more practical basis for evaluating the opportunity.

Ownership is open to different commercial structures. The objective is to identify a proposal that works commercially while protecting the interests of both the developer and the landowners.

The original feasibility study is not published. The professional who prepared it is not identified on this website.

Commercial flexibility

Three structures can be considered.

The final structure will depend on the quality of the proposal, overall value, timing, security, delivery capability and the conditions requested by each party.

Outright sale

A direct acquisition of the development site, subject to agreed commercial terms and due diligence.

Consideration in kind

A structure where the landowners receive agreed completed residential units or other defined consideration in exchange for the land.

Joint or alternative development

Another development structure may be considered where the economics, security and risk allocation are suitable for both sides.
