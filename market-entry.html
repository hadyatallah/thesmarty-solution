Market Entry & Representation

Develop the Cyprus market with practical local commercial support. TSS can help assess the market, develop customers and partners and provide an agreed local commercial presence without immediately building a full local sales operation.

Market Entry Assessment

Understand the market, objective, proposition and likely route.

Entry Development

Research targets, develop customers or partners and test the route.

Representation

Provide agreed continuing local commercial support within explicit authority.

Example: a company wants to test and develop Cyprus before deciding whether to build its own local commercial team.

Representation does not automatically give TSS legal agency, contract-signing authority, price-setting authority, exclusivity, regulatory authority or authority to bind the client.
