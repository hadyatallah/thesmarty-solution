Commercial fit

Strategic Connections

The right connection starts with a clear commercial purpose. TSS identifies and develops relevant commercial connections around a defined objective. The value is in finding, qualifying and progressing the right fit, not selling access to names or contacts.

  • Business to customer
  • Business to distributor
  • Business to strategic partner
  • Owner to developer
  • Developer to opportunity
  • Company to relevant professional ecosystem

A contact is not automatically a connection. A connection is useful only when there is a credible commercial reason for both sides to engage.

TSS does not provide public Commercial Network access, contact credits or arbitrary introductions.

Supporting capability

Business Systems

Use technology where it solves a real business problem. TSS helps businesses improve customer management, communication, follow-up, reporting and workflows. The starting point is the business need, not a predetermined software product.

Understand

Review the operation and the current tools.

Identify

Recommend

Decide whether to improve, integrate, configure or replace anything.

How We Work

A clear route from objective to execution.

The engagement depends on what the business actually needs.

1. Understand the objective

What does the business want to achieve?

2. Establish what is known

Clarify the market, customers, proposition, timing, decision authority and current situation.

3. Identify gaps

If the situation is not ready for execution, Commercial Readiness may be needed first.

4. Define the engagement

Agree scope, responsibilities, boundaries, communication and specialist support.

5. Execute controlled work

Carry out the approved commercial or systems work within the agreed authority.

6. Review and progress

Assess results, current opportunities and the appropriate next commercial step.

Specialist capability where needed

Legal, tax, immigration, accounting, property and other specialist work may require qualified external professionals. TSS coordinates around the commercial objective while the relevant professional remains responsible for their specialist advice or regulated service.
