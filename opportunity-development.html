Opportunity Development

TSS helps assess, prepare and advance defined commercial situations so that the proposition, counterparties and route forward are clear before introductions begin.

Assess

Confirm what exists, what is missing and whether the opportunity is ready.

Structure

Clarify the proposition, roles, information and commercial route.

Position

Prepare a clear market-facing proposition within the approved disclosure level.

Match

Identify relevant counterparties around explicit criteria.

Qualify

Check fit and interest before progressing.

Introduce

Facilitate a controlled introduction where the required authority is in place.

Example: preparing a development opportunity so the proposition, disclosure level and target counterparties are clear before outreach begins.

TSS does not operate an open listing marketplace and does not guarantee investment, a buyer, partner, transaction or other outcome. Regulated matters are routed to the appropriate professional where required.
