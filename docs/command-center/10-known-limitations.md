# Known limitations

- No production deployment or merge. The staged system is not the finished operating system.
- Preview Google sign-in is working. Tablet sign-in does not share the connected browser session. Browser reload ends the CRM session by its existing policy.
- Candidate Version 11 and the shared Agent Ledger are installed. Live ledger reads and daily-brief deduplication passed. Exact-write acceptance and Production release remain open.
- Assistant writes now require the shared gateway. They cannot use a legacy direct-save fallback. Existing manual CRM screens remain separate.
- General model interpretation is the existing service. Full specialist prompt orchestration and adversarial model evaluation are pending.
- Current-source research, Outlook sending, and social provider execution are not connected.
- WhatsApp Business App inbox access is unavailable by design. Sending and conversation logging remain manual.
- Operational provider receipts, heartbeat/backlog and backup evidence are not yet ingested into a complete central health feed.
- Owner is absent from the inspected opportunity schema; no invented ownership or win probability.
- Email ledger availability is not proof of complete mailbox history. Outreach clearance remains conservative.
- Content is draft concepts; final verified assets and publication approval remain manual.
- Native brief jobs and schedule installer are built but not activated. Native event consumer and derived notification center are implemented. Producer hooks, acknowledgment/dismissal and live activation remain pending.
- Ledger scans are linear. Concurrent live callers, ledger growth, retention, backup restore, and crash reconciliation need further acceptance.
- Uncertain CRM writes require inspection of ledger and record. No automatic resend/replay or destructive recovery.
- Android installed-PWA and full end-to-end regression gates remain open.
