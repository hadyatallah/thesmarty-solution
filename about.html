About The Smarty Solution

Business development with a practical route from objective to action.

The Smarty Solution is a Cyprus-based business-development company supporting business growth, market entry, strategic connections and opportunity development. Business systems are used where they genuinely support the commercial objective.

Our position

Business Growth comes first.

TSS works with businesses that need to develop customers, markets, relationships or defined commercial opportunities. We begin with the objective, the market and the commercial situation before deciding what work or systems are justified.

Cyprus is our initial operating strength. Work elsewhere in EMEA is selective and depends on genuine capability, relevant partners, appropriate authority and the scope of the engagement.

Our approach

Start with the commercial objective, not a product.

A company may need better research, clearer targeting, local market development, a qualified commercial connection or a better operating process. It may need technology as part of the solution, but it may not.

Where the existing CRM or operating system already meets the need, TSS can preserve it and focus on process, integration, reporting or other targeted improvements rather than forcing replacement.

Controlled work

Commercial intelligence supports the work. It is not the product.

Research before outreach

TSS uses structured commercial research to identify relevant companies, markets and potential counterparties before action is taken.

Connections with a purpose

We do not sell access to a database or random contacts. A connection should support a defined commercial objective and credible fit.

Selected opportunities

Some opportunities may receive a controlled public summary. They remain part of Opportunity Development, not a public listing marketplace.

Specialist ecosystem

Use the right capability for the right part of the work.

Legal, tax, immigration, accounting, property and other specialist matters may require qualified external professionals. TSS focuses on the commercial next step and coordinates specialist support where it is relevant to the engagement.

The presence of a partner does not automatically imply exclusivity, endorsement, authority or a regulated service from TSS.

Move → Establish → Grow

Specialists may support relocation or establishment. TSS focuses on market entry, representation, customers, partnerships, strategic connections and growth.

What to expect

Clear scope and visible next actions.

  • Objective and scope agreed before substantive work starts
  • Confirmed facts separated from assumptions
  • Confidential information handled through the appropriate level of control
  • Authority and professional boundaries made explicit
  • Practical updates and visible next actions
