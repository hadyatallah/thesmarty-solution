Privacy

Data deletion instructions

How to request access to, correction of, or deletion of information held by The Smarty Solution.

Request deletion

If you have contacted The Smarty Solution Ltd through our website, email, WhatsApp or another business communication channel and want us to delete personal information we control, email info@thesmartysolution.com with the subject Data deletion request.

Tell us which communication channel you used and provide the email address or phone number associated with your enquiry so we can find the relevant records. You do not need to send passwords, identity documents, payment details or other sensitive information with the initial request.

What happens next

We will review your request, take reasonable steps to confirm that it relates to your information and respond through an appropriate contact channel. Where applicable, we will delete or correct personal data within our control, or explain if a legal obligation or another lawful reason requires us to retain particular records. We may ask for limited additional verification if needed to protect your information from unauthorized access or deletion.

WhatsApp and service providers

This request process also covers WhatsApp message events and relevant correspondence held by The Smarty Solution in its own systems, including records received through the WhatsApp Business Platform. WhatsApp and other service providers may also process information under their own terms and data-handling arrangements. You may need to contact those providers separately for information they control.

For more about the information we handle and the reasons for processing it, please review our privacy notice.
