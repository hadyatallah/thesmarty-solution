A simple first step

Start with the requirements.

Send a message from your own email account. There is no online buyer form, automatic registration or promise of a particular property.

01

Describe your interest

Tell us whether you are considering Cyprus or Greece, the property type and any initial questions. Share a broad budget and timeframe only if you wish.

02

03

If a professional introduction is relevant, we explain which organisation would receive your information and ask for appropriate permission first.

What to include in an initial email

Destination · Property type · General budget (optional) · Intended timing (optional) · Questions. Please do not send passports, bank statements, identity documents or other sensitive financial information.

Our role

Business development, research and introductions.

The Smarty Solution LTD is a Cyprus-based business-development and research company. We are not a public property marketplace and we do not guarantee a property, buyer, transaction or investment return.

We do not charge prospective buyers for an initial introduction. Our services are funded by our business clients and TSS may be compensated by them. We will be transparent about the intended recipient and the role of any professional before sharing your details.

TSS does not provide regulated property, investment, financial, tax, legal or immigration advice. Appropriate licensed or qualified professionals must assess any particular transaction or advice.
