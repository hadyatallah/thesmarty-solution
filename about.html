About The Smarty Solution

Business development built around the right opportunities.

A Cyprus-based business development consultancy focused on Cyprus, Lebanon and the wider EMEA region.

Our role

Give the opportunity a clear next step.

We help businesses identify relevant prospects and partners, prepare commercial propositions and coordinate the discussions needed to move an opportunity forward.

Our work also covers the practical systems behind that activity, from customer follow-up to project and service-request workflows.

Our principles

Selective. Practical. Direct.

  • Check suitability before making introductions
  • Set clear expectations and responsibilities
  • Use factual information and protect confidentiality
  • Keep ownership and next actions visible
