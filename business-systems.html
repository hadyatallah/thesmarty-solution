Business Systems

Use technology where it solves a real business problem. TSS helps businesses improve customer management, communication, follow-up, reporting and workflows. The starting point is the business need, not a predetermined software product.

Understand

Review the operation and the current tools.

Identify

Define the actual process or information problem.

Recommend

Decide whether to improve, integrate, configure or replace anything.

Implement

Put the appropriate solution in place where justified.

Possible outcomes include improving the current CRM, preserving a system that already works, configuring another commercial platform, implementing TSS Flow where appropriate, creating a tailored solution, introducing automation or using AI to reduce repetitive work.

Example: an SME manages customers across Outlook, WhatsApp and spreadsheets and needs clearer ownership, follow-up and reporting.

If the existing system already meets the need, TSS will not recommend replacing it simply to introduce another platform.
